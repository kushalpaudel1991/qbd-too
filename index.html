How to Use This Tool

Work through the tabs in order — each stage builds on the data from the one before it.
  1. QTPP — Define the Quality Target Product Profile: the quality attributes and targets your product must meet. Prepopulated with a typical AAV example — edit freely.
  2. CQA & Risk Assessment — List candidate quality attributes and score Impact and Uncertainty on Safety and Efficacy. The tool classifies each as CQA, pCQA, or Non-CQA.
  3. Process Flow — Build your process as unit operations in order, each with its process parameters, units, and Low/High Setpoint operating range.
  4. Linkage — Score how strongly each process parameter affects each CQA/pCQA (0–5).
  5. Classification — Confirm which parameters are Critical (CPP), Key (KPP), or Non-Critical, based on the linkage scores.
  6. FMEA 2A — Score Severity, Occurrence, and Detection for each CPP/KPP to rank process risk.
  7. Characterization Plan — For High-risk parameters only, plan a characterization study (DOE type, range, materials). This tab plans the study — it doesn't run one.
  8. FMEA 2B / Control Strategy — After characterization: record PAR, NOR, Design Space, Action Limits, what to do if a limit is reached, and the broader facility/utilities/personnel/equipment control strategy.
  9. Supportive Studies — Log validation studies that support the process outside per-parameter control: extractables & leachables, viral clearance, sterile filter validation, container closure integrity, hold time studies, and similar.
  10. PPQ Acceptance Criteria — Define acceptance criteria to qualify your process on PPQ batches, for both CQAs and key process parameters.
  11. Module 3 Draft — Assembles a starting CTD Module 3.2.S draft from everything above, with an optional AI polish pass for more natural regulatory prose.

What Each Rating Means

Reference for every scoring scale used across the tool. The same tables also appear as a quick expandable note directly above the relevant table in each tab.

Impact (Safety / Efficacy) — CQA tab

ScoreMeaning
1No plausible effect on patient safety or therapeutic efficacy
2Minor effect — negligible or easily managed
3Moderate effect — noticeable but not severe
4Major effect — significant impact on safety or efficacy
5Critical — severe/life-threatening, or complete loss of therapeutic effect

Uncertainty (Safety / Efficacy) — CQA tab

ScoreMeaning
1Well understood — extensive prior knowledge, literature, or platform data
2Mostly understood — some data available, minor gaps
3Moderately understood — limited data, some assumptions made
4Poorly understood — little direct data, mostly inferred
5Unknown — novel attribute or modality, no data available
Risk Score = Impact × Uncertainty (per axis). Impact of 5 on either axis always yields CQA, regardless of Uncertainty. Otherwise: ≥15 → CQA · 6–14 → pCQA · ≤5 → Non-CQA. Overall classification takes whichever axis is higher-risk.

Linkage Score — Linkage tab

ScoreMeaning
0No relationship — no plausible mechanistic link to this CQA
1Very low impact on this CQA
2Low impact
3Moderate impact
4High impact — likely to measurably shift this CQA
5Very high impact — this parameter is a primary driver of the CQA
A parameter with any linkage score of 4 or 5 is suggested as a CPP in the Classification tab.

Severity, Occurrence, Detection (S-O-D) — FMEA 2A & 2B

ScoreSeverity (effect if parameter deviates)Occurrence (likelihood of deviation)Detection (higher score = harder to detect)
1Negligible effect on the CQARare — highly unlikely given historical controlNear-certain detection — continuous/automated monitoring
2Minor effect, easily correctedUnlikelyHigh detection — reliable in-process testing
3Moderate effect, may need investigation/reworkPossible — occasional excursions observed historicallyModerate detection — periodic sampling, some risk of missing it
4Major effect, likely to cause CQA failureLikely — frequent excursions historicallyLow detection — relies mainly on end-product testing
5Critical — severe/certain CQA failure with safety implicationsAlmost certain without tight controlVery low/no detection — not caught until far downstream
RPN = Severity × Occurrence × Detection. ≥60 → High (characterize) · 20–59 → Medium (verify/monitor) · <20 → Low (no action needed).

Suggested Mode of Study — Characterization Plan tab

2 or more High-risk parameters sharing a unit operation → multivariate DOE suggested (interactions become relevant). Only 1 High-risk parameter → Univariate/OFAT suggested. A starting point, not a rule — use your own judgment.

Parameter Classification — CPP / KPP / Non-CPP

Grouped by unit operation. Suggested from max linkage score (≥4 → CPP). Confirm, override, or mark KPP.
What do CPP / KPP / Non-CPP mean?
CPP — variability meaningfully affects a CQA; must be tightly controlled. KPP — important for process consistency/yield but has no direct CQA link — a judgment call, not derived from linkage. Non-CPP — low linkage to any CQA; standard control is sufficient.

Standard Validation Report Structure (for the full report you'll write outside this tool)
This tool captures the summary-level record for each study — not the full report. When authoring the actual validation report, the following structure is standard across study types (E&L, viral clearance, sterile filtration, CCI, hold time):
  1. Title Page — study title, product, protocol/report number, version, approval signatures
  2. Purpose / Objective — what the study demonstrates
  3. Scope — process steps, materials, product presentations covered (and explicitly what's excluded)
  4. Background & Risk Justification — why the study is needed; reference to the risk assessment driving it
  5. Responsibilities — roles for execution, review, approval
  6. Materials, Equipment & Model Systems — scale-down models, reference standards, challenge organisms/spike materials as applicable
  7. Methodology / Protocol Summary — study design, worst-case justification, sampling plan
  8. Acceptance Criteria — predefined, with justification
  9. Results & Data Summary — data tables, statistical analysis if applicable
  10. Deviations & Investigations — any protocol deviations and their impact assessment
  11. Conclusion — pass/fail disposition against acceptance criteria
  12. References & Appendices — raw data, certificates of analysis, related protocols
